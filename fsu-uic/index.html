Ubiquitous Intelligence & Computing Group

FSU UIC brings together several FSU CS labs working across hardware, systems and networks, artificial intelligence, and human-centered computing. By combining our complementary expertise, we build, deploy, and evaluate ubiquitous intelligent systems that create meaningful value for people and society.

“Building intelligence that lives, learns, and works in the real world.”
